Recovery hatch

Self-custody, verifiable: if the swap network were ever gone for good, the keys of your Bitcoin, EVM, Solana and Monero legs still come from your own seed. Export them once and claim the funds in any external tool.

The export covers every derivation index this wallet used, so funds left on an earlier one stay reachable. The scan plan lists the public addresses to check on each chain when you restore from the recovery phrase alone — it contains no secrets.
